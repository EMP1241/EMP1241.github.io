About

Research questions. Working software.

I am a Geographic Information Science & Technology undergraduate at Texas A&M University, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran, and a geospatial software developer.

My work spans spatial evidence systems, offline and online WebGIS, scientific data workflows, GNSS field analysis, and structured human-and-AI research systems. I create tools that help researchers inspect their data, preserve its context, and carry it into reproducible analysis.

I am a paid research assistant in Torbenson Lab and lead the NWSW GIS Practicum application. My longer-term interests include spatial decision support for site characterization and surface operations, including planetary settings.

Research direction

Tools for scientific decisions.

I’m interested in spatial decision support for site characterization and surface operations, including planetary environments. My current work builds the data handling, field methods, and research software behind that direction.

Across these projects, I focus on keeping original evidence accessible, recording how an analysis was produced, and giving researchers tools they can use in the lab and in the field.

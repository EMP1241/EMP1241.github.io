Composition: wide 16:10 landscape, first-person head-up display with a very subtle visor edge, enough readable terrain to orient the viewer. A tiny suited teammate is faintly visible in shadow in the middle distance and accurately marked at that same position. A destination marker sits on the route ahead. Two different pinned positions are anchored visibly to the ground: one wearer pin and one team pin. Keep the scene open and immediately understandable, with only these four main labels.
Style: polished realistic environment with restrained technical AR concept graphics, professional human-interface design, not a videogame screenshot, not a dramatic movie poster. Sparse thin terrain-depth contour cues only over the nearest dark ground and crater edge. Clear shapes as well as colors differentiate marker types. Cyan diamond for destination, amber person-outline for teammate, violet location pins for shared notes. Text legible at gallery size; leader lines short and unambiguous, labels never overlap.

Constraints: conceptual proposal visualization, not evidence of a running system or NASA deployment. No NASA logo, no copied game imagery, no weapons/crosshairs, no brands, no giant holographic panels, no excessive neon, no invented accuracy claims.

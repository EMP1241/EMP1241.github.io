Deployment scope
The earlier RA 2.3 selective Hub/Library improvements remain deployed and tested in NWSW. This new 2.4 package has not been migrated into that live installation.

What the package makes available

  • 41 method routes with hypothetical worked design cases and eight shared foundations.
  • Four specialist chapters on multi-agent research: coordination, paired evaluations and costs, memory and recovery, and scientific reproduction.
  • Eight statistics chapters covering hierarchical models, missingness, survival, causal estimation, simulation, multiplicity, surveys, and Bayesian checks.
  • 102 source records and 27 local reference texts, linked through 206 indexed records. Publication class, inspected scope, and reuse rights remain explicit.

These counts describe available materials. They do not establish comprehensive disciplinary coverage, complete review of every source, or measured improvements in scientific outcomes. Metadata-only records are distinct from local full-text extracts.

How guidance reaches a participant

The compiler includes the selected methods and their required specialist/source dependencies. Long chapters and papers stay outside routine startup. Local catalog, search, and exact reading use bounded UTF-8 responses with source-hash-bound continuation, so the selected material can be reconstructed completely without silently truncating it. Retrieval makes no network or model calls.

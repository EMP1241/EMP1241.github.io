October 23, 2026 · Development preview · Distribution revision 3 · Scientific library 1.9 · Operational runtime 2.4.0

The practical change

The kit generator now records the team's setup answers, unfinished questions, preparation, and approval before building its assistant kits. A later session can find those decisions and continue the plan. Changed answers retain their history and reopen affected decisions.

Recorded engineering checks

The current setup protocol has 43 baseline questions plus required follow-ups. Fourteen representative omissions were rejected by the supported build process. A synthetic four-role project compiled and passed consistency checks. Individual operator walkthroughs remained pending. Older 42-item setup ledgers are not silently migrated.

Revision 3 updates documentation and the white paper; it retains the prior software and test evidence. It is not a new run of every test or a new assessment of agent research performance.

Package identity

The portfolio independently checked the 5,991,822-byte delivery ZIP, its 509 members, and all 508 content hashes. This verifies the delivered files against their manifest; it does not certify research decisions or operator readiness.

Known setup and onboarding limits

In a later fresh-session and resume trial, both assistants omitted a required startup file before recovering through folder discovery. The saved checklist could be resumed, but the required startup sequence was not followed. That gap remains open.

The build controls check recorded state. They do not authenticate who supplied an approval, judge the adequacy of every answer, or prevent an unrestricted agent from fabricating state. A new participant still needs to learn the project's decisions, evidence, terminology, and unresolved work. Reliable automatic late-stage onboarding has not been demonstrated.

Research guidance and shared Library

The library indexes 134 sources and includes 38 local reference texts. Six new focused appraisals were assigned to relevant method guidance. Source counts are not counts of fully reviewed papers. The runnable teaching cases use invented data; their calculations are distinct from field findings or improved model performance.

The Hub alone publishes additions and corrections to the shared Library after review. Spokes and the Ingestor read approved material and return proposed changes through the Hub. The generic kit does not install a project's backend or grant its permissions.

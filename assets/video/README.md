# PhalAInx introduction clip

`phalainx-introduction.mp4` is the original 10-second branded startup animation from the project's current branding source, `phalainx-splash-video.mp4`. The source branding documentation identifies older archived clips as superseded.

The public file preserves the original H.264 video and AAC audio streams, removes container metadata, and moves the MP4 index forward for web playback. Legacy PhalAInx links containing `?intro=1` start the clip muted in a skippable native dialog; current links from HC and RA open the unlisted studio page directly, with manual replay available. Native controls provide sound and playback; the clip closes on completion. Direct page visits and reduced-motion preferences retain manual playback, with replay available. Failed playback leaves a path to the studio. This is a generated brand animation, separate from the application screenshots and provider integration work.

The visible clip frames were reviewed for private contact details and local paths. Audio was not independently transcribed or reviewed.

## High Command Home recording — October 18, 2026

## Research application demonstrations — October 23, 2026

Three new browser-tab recordings replace the earlier 14-second clips. Pauses and loading gaps were removed by cutting between actual interactions. No camera motion or application behavior was fabricated. All files are silent H.264/YUV 4:2:0 at 30 fps with progressive playback. The homepage and the corresponding project gallery share each file; posters come from the finished recordings.

- `trae-demo-20261023.mp4` (about 7.8 seconds, 1600 × 724): TRAE v1.0.0, using temporary imports of the public NOAA/NCEI Fish Creek Trail files `ca609.rwl` and `ca609.crn`. Shows map zoom, year-range changes, an 11-year moving mean, selection of measured series FCT01A, restoration of the full site mean, and the enlarged chart. The minimap remains collapsed. Short interaction sequences are played at half or two-thirds speed for readability, with idle intervals removed. This is an edited feature demonstration, not a performance benchmark or a validated chronology result.
- `tvae-demo-20261023.mp4` (6 seconds, 1600 × 724): TVAE v1.3.0, showing Indian Ocean context, Kairei and Edmond Field records, map movements, and selected-site summary charts. Bird (2003) PB2002 tectonic boundaries are enabled. Uses public InterRidge records with no imported user data and no write-back sidecar. Source labels and map attribution are retained. Actual zoom and tile transitions remain visible.
- `mars-demo-20261023.mp4` (8 seconds, 1280 × 940): Mars Explorer v0.9.2 from an isolated fresh portable preview. Shows multiple pans, zoom changes, and switching from the sol 3049 Mont Mercou Navcam panorama to the cliff-side sol 3053 view. Cropped around the running look-around dialog, retaining its station selector, source description, controls, heading cues and coverage notice. The source imagery is NASA/JPL-Caltech. Mosaic seams, angular guides, and missing-coverage areas remain visible. Separate rover stations are not represented as continuous camera travel; the panorama viewer is separate from orbital-data 3D terrain.

Raw recordings and superseded public videos remain in private working files outside the published repository. The replacement clips were checked for desktop windows, private contacts, credentials, and private project files. The discarded first Mars take followed a duplicate tab and was not published. Gallery playback continues to respect reduced motion, the site-wide effects control, and manual playback ownership.

NWSW Spatial Evidence Workbench

A local research workbench connecting original sources, spatial records, human review, and reproducible investigations.

The problem

Research teams need to connect mapped records and conclusions to the exact sources, revisions, and review decisions behind them. Integrating documents, tables, GIS files, and contributor packages requires preserving that context through intake, review, and analysis.

My role

As project lead and application owner, I coordinate research contributions and own the development, integration, operation, and maintenance of the workbench. The research is teamwork; original contributors and sources retain attribution.

Data and method

The application preserves original files and source identities, validates incoming contributor packages, stores spatial records in PostgreSQL/PostGIS, and records explicit review states. Saved investigations and exports retain their source versions, filter scope, methods, and review history.

The interface uses TypeScript and Leaflet, supported by a Python/FastAPI backend. Bundled terrain supports offline map access.

What I found

The implemented workflow keeps file acceptance, automated checks, and human research review separate. Original sources and review history remain traceable through mapped investigations and reproducible exports.

The broader research assessment is ongoing. This case study covers the implemented application and its evidence workflow.

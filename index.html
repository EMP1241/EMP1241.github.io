I’m Brandon Santor, a Geographic Information Science & Technology undergraduate at Texas A&M, a Marine Corps veteran, and a geospatial software developer.

I process complex tree-ring measurements and climate records for inspection and downstream analysis, map deep-ocean vents and geomicrobiology context, and reconstruct Martian landscapes in 3D from orbital data. I also build spatial decision tools, investigate smartphone positioning error, and design research systems that keep a team’s evidence and methods connected.

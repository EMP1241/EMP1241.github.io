The researcher behind the tools

I turn complex research into tools people can use.

I’m Brandon Santor, an undergraduate studying geographic information science and technology at Texas A&M, a Marine Corps veteran, and a geospatial software developer.

I prepare tree-growth measurements and climate records for comparison, map deep-ocean vents against seafloor relief and plate boundaries, and reconstruct bounded sections of the Martian landscape in 3D from orbital elevation and imagery. Across these projects, I build tools that link original sources, spatial data, and reviewable analysis.

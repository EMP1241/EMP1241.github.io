## Executive summary

Research Architect provides an operating framework for human research teams working with AI. It helps design new work or organize an existing project around its retained evidence, decisions and constraints. The Architect proposes methods and feasible assignments; the accountable human chooses the design, authorities and consequential changes. A reviewed design becomes individual role kits with project instructions, selected methodological guidance, local helpers and continuity records. The New World Screwworm (NWSW) deployment supplied an active setting in which these mechanisms were used and refined. Its shared Library retains approved findings, methods, provenance, artifacts and use limits. The Hub alone publishes additions and corrections; Spokes and Ingestor read approved evidence directly within their permissions and return new contributions through the configured review process. The current RA 2.6.1 factory supplies executable setup-environment verification, review of the exact written plan before kit creation, scoped delivery and retained approval evidence. Local semantic checks examine declared data relationships, and claim-to-evidence checks reconcile stated quantities and quotations with pinned inputs. Incremental Library preparation, compatible retrieval and durable publication records support reviewed sharing. Deployment observations and software checks retain their respective scopes. ## 1 Introduction

AI-assisted research often spans several people, model sessions and collections of files. A useful answer becomes difficult to carry forward when the next session lacks its scope, supporting evidence, decisions or unresolved work. Teams also need to identify the current artifact, distinguish review from delivery and recover work after a session or machine changes.

Existing agent literature supplies useful design context. OpenAI describes models, tools and instructions as foundational components and discusses orchestration and guardrails. AutoGen demonstrates configurable conversations combining agents, tools and human input. Anthropic describes the coordination, evaluation and reliability work behind a deployed multi-agent research system. These sources inform the architectural context; their performance results are not evidence of RA performance. [1] [2] [3]

RA addresses the operating structure surrounding a research task. The Architect helps establish questions, assignments, methods, dependencies and review requirements. A compiler produces individual role kits and coordinated folders. Local helpers preserve state, inspect artifacts, prepare reviewed exchanges and support recovery. The selected coding-agent host supplies model inference and actual file/code access. These tools extend the workflow available to the agent without changing its model weights. Human operators retain responsibility for scientific judgments and consequential decisions. [E7]

## 2 System architecture

RA combines a reviewed project design, human-operated roles, persistent records and a shared project Library. Figure 1 separates Hub read/write access from Spoke and Ingestor read-only access. Only the Hub publishes additions and corrections. RA's scientific-methods collection supplies research guidance to the factory; the Library retains approved project findings for contextual reuse. Each host installation needs its own tested startup, storage and transfer configuration. [E3] [E4] [E5] [E6]

### Project design and role kits

The Hub issues bounded tasks, reviews contributions and validation results, obtains final human approval and uses its read/write Library authority to publish additions and corrections. Spokes conduct assigned work with their human operators, who accept and approve exact submissions. The Ingestor receives Hub-accepted work, validates it and reproduces results when needed, then returns its findings to the Hub. Generated kits carry these responsibilities, selected methods and continuity records. Generation does not grant remote access or establish scientific approval. [E6]

The Architect begins with the research question and the claim the team wants to support. Its methodological knowledge base combines research-design guidance, specialist chapters, worked cases, source appraisals and routes to original publications. Substantial reusable depth covers multi-agent behavior and evaluation, causal inference, hierarchical and Bayesian analysis, missing data, uncertainty, analysis selection and generalization. The Architect uses this material to examine observations, measurement limits, units of analysis, assumptions and appropriate checks. Method selection remains tied to the actual question, available evidence and qualified human review. Relevant original sources and project-specific guidance support consequential applications. [E4] [E11] [E19]

Specialized routes include multi-agent systems and AI/ML, planetary science and astrobiology, GIS and remote sensing, geochronology and geophysics, and computational omics. Standard routes cover experiments, observational studies, surveys, systematic reviews, qualitative inquiry, mixed methods and statistics. Across 41 routes, shared methods connect research design to worked analyses, conflicting evidence and target-population generalization. Instrument-specific procedures, professional standards and specialized questions receive project-specific sourcing and review. [E4] [E19]

The design also accounts for who has the expertise and time to conduct and review the work. The workload record includes available hours, methodological competence, deadlines, dependencies, anticipated rework and review burden. AI/runtime limits, such as tool availability, quotas, persistent storage and budget, enter the assessment when they affect feasibility. The Architect can recommend reassigning work, reducing scope, changing the schedule or obtaining focused specialist review. The human authority chooses the configuration; estimates retain uncertainty, and adding agents is not assumed to add useful capacity. [E11]

The human lead establishes project scope, decision authority, evidence-use boundaries and approval responsibilities with the team and applicable institutional requirements. Researchers remain responsible for their submissions and the access they control. The Hub coordinates work within those decisions; it does not acquire authority to expand access, change a study's consequential methods or accept a finding merely by recommending it. The Ingestor reports what it checked and reproduced, including unresolved discrepancies. Local software verifies defined technical conditions; qualified humans judge scientific adequacy and authorize consequential use. [E6] [E11]

Evidence boundaries have a research purpose. An item may be outside the agreed question, awaiting verification, restricted to a particular audience, or intentionally withheld until an independent first assessment is recorded. Such limits can protect confidentiality, preserve a valid comparison or prevent an unreviewed claim from becoming a shared assumption. Methodologically necessary information must not be concealed to manufacture a preferred result. The authorized human decides changes to these boundaries; the Hub records and implements the applicable scope, subject to actual host and storage permissions. [E11]

Research assignments, submissions, disagreements, proposed corrections and release decisions pass through the configured Hub routes. Approved Library lookups can occur directly. A participant's private conversation or local files become shared project evidence only when captured and delivered through an authorized route; the Hub does not automatically observe every device or conversation. [E6] [E11]

### Local agent workspace and verified setup

RA and its kits operate in an agent environment that can access persistent local project files and execute code on the machine. Codex, Antigravity and Claude Code are examples, rather than a provider whitelist. Uploading a ZIP to ordinary browser-only chat or running code in a temporary sandbox does not establish the required working environment. Manual file exchange and recovery remain useful between suitable local workspaces. [E7] [E8]

After the environment and Python responses, a bounded preflight requires an agent file challenge, saved readback and a result from an independent Python process. The environment respondent explicitly confirms the actual local agent workspace and persistent host. Missing, stale or withdrawn evidence blocks later intake and all supported kit-generation routes. File/code access is machine-checked; physical locality and long-term host durability are human-confirmed. The same saved evidence is rechecked on resume and before publication without repeatedly launching the worker. [E7]

### Model choices and operating costs

The Architect records the models and reasoning settings each operator can actually select, then recommends a default for each role. The plan includes the task rationale, expected cost tradeoff, available alternatives, switching conditions, an acceptance check and a retry limit. A single available model is valid; unavailable alternatives and unknown settings remain explicit. Recommendations based on documentation, operator experience or initial judgment are marked PROVISIONAL. MEASURED recommendations require comparable trial evidence and a dated reference. The compiler checks the record's structure and bindings, not the truth of a model-performance claim. [E11]

At runtime, an agent can recommend a stronger or lighter option under the recorded conditions. The human decides whether to switch, and actual access and provider permissions still apply. The agent reports its current model or reasoning setting only when the host exposes it or the operator confirms it. Routine integrity checks and diagnostics can run locally without another model call. Total cost assessment includes setup, review, failed attempts, saving and restoration; unavailable usage or billing remains unknown. Persistent local project storage does not imply local model inference: the selected host and provider determine where requests are processed and which material is transmitted. [E11]

### Written plan and scoped delivery

The finalized design produces a shared-team PDF and a Hub PDF. The shared edition contains the permitted team context and explicitly selected coordinator sections; the Hub edition also carries its approved coordinator records and setup responsibilities. Role-specific handoffs preserve the storage, transport, permissions, model plan, owners and pending actions relevant to each recipient. Generation does not configure cloud accounts or grant permissions. [E7]

Before kits can be created, the accountable human must approve the exact bytes of both plan editions, bound to the configuration and source snapshot. Compilation copies those reviewed PDFs unchanged and attaches separate review receipts. Changed configuration, intake or plan artifacts require renewed review; explicit refusal blocks creation. Existing approval events remain in history. The current build also requires the verified setup-environment receipt, so recorded prose alone cannot satisfy the local-capability requirement. [E7]

### Local semantic validation and claim concordance

Local Python checks bind CSV evidence to declared records, populations, periods, variables and units. They test types, missing-value codes, identities, allowed values, ranges, date windows and count/denominator relationships. Join checks expose ambiguous keys and projected row multiplication. Reports preserve original data, exact input identities, complete violation counts and labeled source-record samples; missing observations remain separate from zero. [E20]

Claim checks bind exact final-text passages to source artifacts and reconstruct declared counts, sums, means or record proportions. They compare complete numbers, explicit rounding tolerance, units, scope and required qualifications, and verify exact quotations. Failed semantic inputs block dependent numerical claims. Unchanged reports can be verified and reused, with complete details available through bounded continuation. These local tools make no additional model or network calls. Investigators judge scientific meaning, source suitability and acceptance. [E20]

### Persistent context and source retrieval

Supported startup resolves the designated current checkpoint rather than choosing a directory by timestamp. Successor snapshots preserve their base and record changed state. The current generated startup uses selective retrieval: it selects the declared source scope for the requested intent, emits each required control once and replaces only exact repeated original lines with backward references. Before publication, it reconstructs every selected source byte and checks the source bindings. Original records remain available. The classic multipart path, with repeated governing controls, remains an explicit alternative. These controls preserve selected text and detect missing or altered parts; they do not establish that a model has understood or retained it. [E11] [E12]

Ordinary questions use bounded lookup of the relevant records. Routine message or diagnostic operations have scoped routes; resolving the current checkpoint or checking files on disk does not require emitting every file into the model's context. Within the same conversation, compact reuse is permitted only after fresh local source and selector checks and explicit confirmation that all required selected context was read and remains available. A saved index cannot prove that retention. A new session, context loss, uncertainty or a changed checkpoint, source, instruction, reader or sealed Library configuration requires renewed context output. Complete restoration is an explicit operation over its declared scope. [E11]

The active context window is the material available to the model for its current response. Project records persist outside that window. If the host condenses older messages or a researcher opens another conversation, restoration retrieves the current controls, state and task-relevant evidence from those records. RA does not enlarge the provider's context window, guarantee unlimited cumulative retention or reload the entire project at every prompt. It also does not retrain the model's neural-network weights when the team corrects a finding. Saved decisions, mistakes and their resolutions become retrievable project knowledge; their correct use still requires checking. [E11]

The persistence protocol calls for saving at meaningful milestones, review decisions, handoffs, session closure and before expected context loss. A researcher can ask, for example, "Save my spot, record what changed and what remains unresolved, and tell me exactly where to resume." This is a natural-language instruction to invoke the save workflow, not a universal hardcoded command or an automatic guarantee. The agent updates the research and workflow state, session handoff, index and changed linked records; it preserves the previous confirmed checkpoint, verifies the saved bytes and reports the exact resume point and pending work. A failed or partial save remains PENDING. "Resume from the saved checkpoint" then requires identity and version checks and reconciliation of intervening changes. Information left only in an unsaved conversation remains vulnerable, and closing a session does not close its research workstream. [E11]

Retrieving the relevant original passages brings that task input to 3,716 characters, before the answer is added. The answer retains the approved exclusion interval and the pending sensitivity check. Other questions can require other records. These illustrative counts do not measure RA restoration or show the entire archive compressed into a short summary. Exact duplicate removal during restoration and conditional reuse of already-retained context are separate mechanisms, described above and in the measured results. [E11] [E12]

The shared project Library is a governed repository of approved evidence, findings, methods and artifacts. The Hub has read/write authority and alone publishes additions and corrections through the human-approved review route. Spokes and Ingestor have read-only access within their approved scope. The Hub controls reviewed audience selections and handles requests for held material; researchers and agents reuse published knowledge with its provenance and use limits. Per-role setup records identify Library status, configuration, responsible people and remaining actions. This supplies the shared-learning mechanism as project knowledge accumulates. [E5] [E6] [E14]

Discovery preserves canonical artifact and version identities, original locations, dependencies, review status and correction relationships. Membership references let one canonical item appear in several collections without duplicating the research output. Extracted packages retain their relative paths and parent bindings. A Hub-published correction can be exposed through both the canonical record and its discovery routes, helping agents recognize which version and limitations apply. [E5]

The outward availability labels are Available in Library and Request from Hub. Already-approved files and verified aliases remain usable within their original scope without repeating approval; safe metadata for held work directs a request to the Hub. Shared views use reviewed audience selections and omit restricted internal records. The Library is distinct from RA's bundled scientific-methods collection: the former retains a team's evolving evidence and outputs, while the latter supplies reusable research guidance for configuring kits. [E4] [E5]

A researcher can discover reviewed catalog metadata for held work without receiving its payload or permission to use the finding. A request should identify the exact artifact and version, explain the research dependency and intended use, and retain audience and other relevant limits. The Hub checks whether the item is located, cleared, still under review or unavailable. It can coordinate remaining checks and recommend prioritizing review or release; the human lead decides consequential changes to assignments, deadlines or access. In the inspected NWSW configuration, requests use the participant's existing Hub route and inform Brandon's review priorities. Fulfillment is not guaranteed, and restricted metadata or unlocated acquisition leads need not appear in shared discovery. ### Evidence review and decision traceability

The Hub defines a bounded task with scope, inputs, methods, artifacts and acceptance criteria. The Spoke performs it, and its human approves the exact submission. The Hub reviews and accepts it for Ingestor validation and any needed reproduction. The Ingestor returns checks, discrepancies and limits. The Hub obtains final human approval, publishes the exact version with its use scope, and verifies publication. Failed checks return for correction and repeat affected reviews. Other roles propose Library corrections; only the Hub applies and publishes them, preserving prior versions and decisions. [E6]

Human-agent discussions can preserve the decisions, alternatives, objections and reasoning behind an output. The supported reviewer-bundle helper binds supplied conversation files and their declared session coverage to the research package for Hub review. All relevant supplied discussion remains available as evidence; summaries do not replace the transcripts. The helper does not automatically capture every host message, tool call or attachment, so missing coverage must be declared. Transcripts stay in restricted review storage; publishing a research finding does not automatically disclose its raw conversations. [E6]

### Applying the established 10th man principle

RA applies the established 10th man principle as a deliberate challenge to apparent consensus. The familiar formulation assigns someone to examine the possibility that the prevailing conclusion is wrong. Professional discussion uses the name, and the underlying practice of devil's advocacy has a longer history. In Psychology of Intelligence Analysis, the CIA's Center for the Study of Intelligence describes assigning a reviewer to develop a minority interpretation and expose assumptions behind competing views. RA adapts that approach to coordinated human-AI research; it does not claim to originate the principle. [12] [14]

Every new Hub, Spoke and Ingestor kit now carries explicit instructions for Hub-controlled disclosure and preservation of the initial assessment. Before a Spoke receives another Spoke's output, the sender and recipient must verify the current Hub authorization, recipient, task/use and exact output/version or approved collection scope, together with required human authority. Existing valid releases and Hub-approved Library publication remain usable within their recorded scope. For a comparison task, the agent must save and verify its own initial assessment before seeing the other interpretation. Later comparison preserves that initial version and records actual exposure and revisions. The accountable human makes the acceptance decision. [E6] [E11] [E15]

Operational awareness means knowing each agent's assignment, the evidence and interpretations it has seen, its authorized access, current decisions and conditions for stopping or escalating. The Hub coordinates that awareness through task, disclosure, handoff and review records. Every kit's startup instructions route to the shared rules. Its review and handoff fields retain the release, inspected evidence, initial conclusions, uncertainty, objections, exposure and exact saved assessment reference. Resume retrieves the relevant task records; it does not load the entire review history. A failed save leaves comparison pending. An already-exposed assessment cannot be relabeled as an unexposed first assessment. These operating instructions and records preserve existing approvals and closed work; host access controls and actual compliance remain separately established. [E6] [E11] [E15]

Focused delivery checks generated one fresh kit for each of the three roles. They confirmed both startup entry points, review and handoff fields, and preservation of both requirements through classic and selective context restoration. The existing integrity check detected an altered sealed instruction and stopped restoration. Prior exact-PDF review bytes and retained source fixtures were preserved. These completed checks establish instruction delivery and restoration in the exercised paths. Continued behavioral evaluation can examine how models apply the procedure and how project records capture actual exposure. [E15]

The responsible human and Hub define the challenge question, appropriate evidence access, reviewer or review mechanism, stopping conditions and decision authority. The reviewer tests credible alternatives and may conclude that the original result survives scrutiny. After an independent assessment, the Hub may authorize scoped comparison while preserving dissent for the human's final decision. A leading assignment, accidental disclosure or unauthorized access can compromise independence without a deliberate human decision to seek agreement. The process stops when the issue is resolved, available evidence cannot distinguish the alternatives or the authorized budget is exhausted; unresolved concerns remain visible. [E11]

For consequential findings, the governance guidance recommends qualified independent review or an appropriate challenge mechanism when feasible. The record identifies any overlap between the Hub operator, contributor reviewer and final authority. Where staffing calls for documented self-review, the authorized human records the checks, remaining uncertainty and actual decision, subject to applicable institutional requirements. This keeps small-team work practical while making the basis of review inspectable. Continued evaluation can measure adherence to the safeguards and their contribution to error detection, evidence quality, review cost and final decisions. [13] [E11]

### Outside research and focused specialist review

A published study, agency dataset, external analysis or commissioned report can be brought forward by the lead, a researcher or the Hub within the project's authorized scope. Existing task, validation, review and publication mechanisms support bounded review of that material. The team's responsible human authorizes its submission and intended use; original authorship, access restrictions and provenance remain attached. Today this route uses the established mechanisms with project-specific preparation. A dedicated external importer and further end-to-end qualification are areas for continued development. [E11]

The handoff needs the exact source and edition, citation or stable locator, relevant supplements, the specific claim or question to check, and available reproduction materials. Where applicable, those materials include data, code, parameters, environment requirements and expected outputs. Missing data, inaccessible methods and unresolved dependencies remain explicit. The Hub sets acceptance criteria within the approved task, the Ingestor reports checks and actual reproduced results, and the Hub obtains the required human decision before publishing an exact version and its permitted use. Reviewing citations and methods is different from reproducing results. If reproduction cannot be completed, the record must state not reproduced or blocked, with the reason; qualified evidence use may still be possible. [E6] [E11]

A focused outside specialist can review a method or finding without receiving a new Spoke kit. Using ordinary document-authoring tools, the Hub agent can prepare a readable packet containing the review question, exact versioned material, necessary project context, evidence and methods, known limitations and requested checks. Material deliberately withheld for an independent first pass is identified in the review record. The specialist's returned assessment records their identity, expertise relevant to the question, checks actually performed, conclusions, disagreement and any remaining conditions. The Hub preserves that return and routes consequential decisions to the authorized human. [E11]

RA supplies a review-record template and workflow guidance; a readable packet can be assembled from those records. PDF or Word export uses the tools available in the host. A dedicated automatic specialist-packet exporter remains a future automation step. Delivery, completed specialist review and the human's decision retain separate records. Focused consultation also provides an option when a full new participant-and-agent onboarding would exceed the project's available capacity, as discussed in Section 7. [E11]

### Versioned handoffs and recovery

Local helpers stage payloads, validate configured routes and retain receipts. Authorized host connectors perform remote transfer during active sessions. Preparation, delivery, reading, adoption, research acceptance and permitted downstream use remain distinct. Backup helpers verify selected project contents and captured external files, retain explicit recovery metadata and exercise restoration. Storage and transport configuration remain responsibilities of the deployment.

### Diagnostics and repair coordination

The Doctor diagnoses the explicitly selected kit and its configured components. It leaves the sealed kit unchanged while writing external reports and diagnostic-operation records. Its seven scopes cover identity, context, storage, messages, Library, backup and recovery. Compact findings retain complete evidence through bounded continuation. A clean result establishes the checked technical state, rather than scientific approval, and diagnosis does not itself authorize repair. [E7]

The Hub can coordinate a repair and relay the configured human authority's actual, bounded instruction to a Spoke. The Spoke's agent performs permitted work locally, subject to its operator/host controls and the recovery tool's checks. RA supplies no general Hub-issued repair capability or remote-control channel. For example, lock recovery requires the inspected lock hash, the same host and a proven dead owner process. Checkpoint recovery retains its clean-base, review and publication requirements. This preserves practical maintenance coordination without treating a Hub message as a tool grant. [E7]

## 6 Research basis and methodological applicability

Multi-agent design requires explicit coordination and verification. Cemri and colleagues distinguish system-design, inter-agent alignment and task-verification failures. RA uses this taxonomy to examine pending questions, task drift and incomplete completion checks. Applying the taxonomy is an engineering interpretation, not evidence that RA shares the study's failure rates or has eliminated those failure modes. [4]

AI Agents That Matter argues for cost-aware evaluation, credible simpler baselines and holdouts matched to the intended generalization. RA adopts these considerations when planning comparisons: report the full retry policy and task denominator, and distinguish emitted characters from measured tokens and billed cost. The appraisal uses the explicitly identified 2024 author edition; it does not treat later publication as evidence of a new RA experiment. [5]

CORE-Bench evaluates computational reproduction using supplied code and data. Its inspected author version and separate journal publication identity are distinguished in the references. Reproducing an expected output is a useful verification target, but it does not independently establish the truth of a study's measurements or causal interpretation. RA therefore records file integrity, execution, operator rehearsal and scientific review separately. [6]

Preregistration guidance distinguishes prospective plans from choices made after seeing outcomes. RA's preparation approval concerns kit creation, not preregistration of a study. Applicable research plans should record timing, prior outcome exposure and deviations while preserving exploratory work under an accurate label. [7]

Clustered-inference guidance explains why observations within the same project or task group cannot automatically be counted as independent evidence. RA's workflow-evaluation case distinguishes all-attempt outcomes from completed-pair diagnostics; statistical inference must respect the design's independent units and few-cluster limits. The teaching case is an application of methodological principles, not a new empirical validation of them. [8]

Leakage analysis requires separation through preprocessing and evaluation, legitimate predictors and a test distribution that matches the scientific claim. RA's analysis-selection guidance carries these checks into model comparison. Nested selection or a held-out split does not repair inappropriate measurements or an unsupported target-population claim. [9]

FAIR principles emphasize identifiable, described and reusable research objects; practical scientific-computing guidance supports preserving raw inputs, processing records and change history. These sources inform RA's provenance and handoff design. RA's actual preservation and restore results are supported by its retained deployment receipts, not inferred from citing those principles. The 10th man principle provides a recognizable description of deliberate challenge, while devil's advocacy and alternative analysis supply its methodological context. Heuer describes examining conflicting interpretations; Rieber and Thomason caution that intuitive confidence in analytic techniques can exceed the evidence for their effectiveness. The latter discuss mixed research on assigned devil's advocacy, including the possibility of reinforcing the original position. The professional use of the tenth-man label establishes its recognition, not a universally standardized protocol or a validated RA intervention. These sources support evaluating the safeguards rather than assuming that a named challenger guarantees better judgment. [12] [13] [14]

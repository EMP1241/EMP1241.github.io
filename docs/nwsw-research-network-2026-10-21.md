# NWSW research network diagram

The owner clarified the operational sequence on October 21, 2026:

1. Pod human review.
2. Hub human review.
3. Ingestor verification and reproduction within the authorized scope.
4. Return to the same Hub for final human acceptance.
5. Approved Library material becomes shared context for subsequent work.

These are three checks followed by acceptance, not newly defined formal governance gates. The diagram describes the process; it does not assert that every contribution has completed reproduction successfully.

The continuous return arrow represents retrieval and reuse of reviewed context. Labels sit beside connectors instead of obscuring them. The knowledge nodes represent sources, methods, decisions, and corrections; they are not model neurons or an assertion of neural-network training.

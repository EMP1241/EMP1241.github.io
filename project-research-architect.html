Research Architect

Research Architect is my kit-building framework for human teams. It converts a reviewed project design into individual AI agent kits with a named human operator, bounded workstream, methods, dependencies, and concrete starting action. Each person operates their own configured agent within a shared research system.

The Hub coordinates shared work and releases; Spokes carry out assigned research; the Ingestor organizes incoming evidence and technical checks. My Python generator builds the role kits and coordinated working folders. A lightweight HTML evidence interface supports inspection of incoming material and its history.

Research behind the architecture

The design is documented in a technical white paper and comparative literature review. I drew on thousands of sources to establish the AI’s expectations for rigorous research. Peer-reviewed research, research preprints, and standards inform its approach to multi-agent coordination, scientific provenance, persistent context, and human oversight.

FAIR data principles, W3C PROV, TRUST principles, and AI risk-management work provide part of that foundation. The scientific library includes 16 method modules. My revised technical white paper documents the architecture and an operational NWSW case study, including retained execution receipts, recovery checks, and RA 2.1 release verification.

From an assignment to a verified handoff

Contributor packages carry manifests and SHA-256 checks to verify file identity. Version checks reject stale starting states. Destination-local staging keeps partial outputs separate; exclusive publication and readback checks guard against conflicting revisions in the managed workflow. Review, delivery, and acknowledgement retain separate records so the team can tell what was approved, sent, and received.

I used Research Architect to generate the NWSW coordination system, configured its deployment, and supported onboarding eight people. The independent Spatial Evidence Workbench is a separate application; the RA white paper assesses the framework and generated Hub workflow.

Research that survives a new session

The startup workflow resolves the designated current checkpoint. Successor snapshots preserve earlier state, while a routed operating dictionary points back to original evidence and prior decisions. Multipart context restoration carries large research histories across bounded outputs while preserving source text and governing controls.

Backup tooling verifies copied bytes, preserves selected project files and downloaded artifacts, and supports isolated restoration. In the October 16 inventory, the deployed Hub tracked 262 distinct transfer IDs: 98 incoming and 164 outgoing tickets. Retained dispatch receipts document actual deliveries separately.

The studio

PhalAInx is my independent software development studio. I lead its product direction, research logic, system architecture, and implementation. The studio develops tools for two different settings: a human research team where each member has an assigned AI assistant, and one person coordinating several AI models across projects.

Two products, two working contexts

Research Architect turns a reviewed research plan into an AI assistant kit for each team member, with shared methods, source context, human review, and handoffs. I used it to generate the New World Screwworm project’s coordination system.

High Command is the studio’s personal desktop workspace for managing work across AI tools. Its Windows alpha now stores briefs, imported transcripts, manual tasks, milestones, and relationships locally, with tested recovery paths. Live model execution is a later stage.

These products solve different coordination problems under the PhalAInx studio. Explore Research Architect → · Explore High Command →

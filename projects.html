01 / Featured · Research leadership, mapping & software

Explore the New World Screwworm Project

I lead a Texas A&M student team studying reports of New World Screwworm, a parasitic fly that affects livestock, for the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA). The study covers Guatemala, Mexico, and the southern United States. I recovered missing dates so reports could be compared over time, checked conflicting records, and connected livestock and transport figures to the places they describe. I also test how uncertain locations affect our maps and build the software the team uses to review its evidence. The case study walks through that work and what we have learned so far.

  • Research leadership
  • Spatial data engineering
  • Source reconciliation
  • Reproducible GIS builds

02 / Research ArchitectPhalAInx framework · Creator & system designer

Research Architect (RA)

I built a framework that gives each research team member’s AI assistant a defined job, working instructions, and reviewed sources. It saves decisions and unfinished work so the team can pick up where it left off. I used it to establish the New World Screwworm team’s coordination system.

Mars Explorer

I lead a five-person team and built the tools that put NASA’s position records, images, and elevation measurements together. Visitors can explore selected Martian terrain, look around rover panoramas, inspect scientific records, and browse 200 images in the public browser edition or downloadable Windows application, both released as version 1.0.0.

High Command (HC)

Spatial Evidence Workbench

01 / Featured · Research leadership, mapping & software

Explore the New World Screwworm Project

I lead a Texas A&M student research team conducting New World Screwworm analysis for the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA). Our study spans Guatemala, Mexico, and the southern United States. I recover missing dates, resolve inconsistencies in source records, connect livestock figures to geographic areas, and reconstruct transportation evidence. I also test environmental data and geographic assumptions and build the software supporting the team’s research. Follow the case study to see the problems I investigated, the work I completed, and what it made possible.

  • Research leadership
  • Spatial data engineering
  • Source reconciliation
  • Reproducible GIS builds
